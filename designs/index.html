The actor model

A two-signal axis plus shape, never a rainbow. Survives grayscale.

You

round identicon · "where you are" · caution · focus ring · armed confirm

Typography

Schibsted Grotesk (UI) · JetBrains Mono (money / addresses, tabular). Hierarchy from weight + size + color.

Spacing — the 4px grid

Gaps, padding, margins. space-2 is the sole sub-grid half-step.
